无尽星轨 / Endless Startrail — 广告版隐私政策

中文隐私政策

适用范围与联系

本政策适用于《无尽星轨》Google 版当前离线版本，由个人开发者日生青提供。隐私与客服联系邮箱：junyuy82@gmail.com。

本机处理的信息

关卡进度、装备、芯片、研究、游戏内货币、奖励记录及音量等设置保存在应用自己的本机目录，用于恢复游戏进度。本地说明的已读状态也保存在本机。

游戏在本机读取屏幕尺寸、安全区域及系统运行环境，用于画面、输入和声音适配。游戏不主动将这些信息、游戏存档或运行日志上传给开发者。

主动联系

您主动发送邮件时，邮箱地址、问题描述及您自愿附带的截图或日志将用于回复及排查。请不要发送密码、身份证件或无关敏感信息。游戏不自动发送邮件或附件。

反馈资料仅在处理请求和必要安全核查所需期限内保留，无继续保留必要时删除，法律另有要求的除外。您可通过上述邮箱提出查阅、更正或删除反馈资料的请求。

本地保存与删除

游戏记录保存在本机，直至您清除应用数据、卸载应用或系统清理。当前版本未提供云端备份，请勿将应用更新、换机或重新安装视为存档一定可恢复。

Android 可通过系统设置中的应用存储页面清除本应用数据；此操作会删除本地进度。游戏设置中的“隐私与服务 → 本地数据管理”提供说明。当前版本不建立游戏账号，因此没有游戏账号注销流程。

适用版本

本节仅适用于提供 Google AdMob 自愿激励广告的 Google 版，包括使用官方测试广告的开发版本。无广告的离线版本不加载这些 SDK。本节替代离线政策中的“无广告、无联网”描述，其余本地存档、联系和删除说明继续适用。

Google 广告与同意管理

广告版本使用 Google Mobile Ads SDK 和 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP），在请求广告前检查隐私选择，并在适用时显示 Google 提供的同意表单。您可以不观看广告并继续正常游玩；在设置中的“广告隐私选择”入口可以重新查看可用的隐私选项。

阅读游戏内说明不代替广告同意。无法确认可请求广告时，广告保持不可用。非个性化广告也可能处理设备、网络和广告交互信息。

SDK 处理的数据及用途

根据设备、地区、SDK 版本及隐私选择，Google 广告服务可能处理 IP 地址（可用于推断大致位置）、广告标识符或其他设备/应用标识符、广告展示和点击等交互、诊断与性能信息以及同意选择，用于投放广告、衡量效果、防范欺诈及提供服务。广告版本需要网络连接。

我们不向广告请求附加玩家姓名、电子邮件、本地存档或自定义玩家 ID，不启用 Firebase Analytics 或第三方广告聚合。广告 SDK 的数据会传输至 Google，可能在您所在国家或地区以外处理。Google 的保留期限和隐私权操作见其政策；清除游戏存档不保证删除广告服务已处理的数据。

奖励与选择

只有您主动点击奖励按钮才展示激励广告。广告未加载、播放失败、提前退出或未收到有效奖励回调时，不扣除游戏货币，也不发放该次广告奖励。自然恢复体力和正常游戏入口保留。

可在 Android 的广告隐私设置中管理或删除广告标识符；具体选项取决于系统版本。SDK 在同意和其他适用隐私限制允许的范围内请求广告。

儿童与广告资格

广告功能不会仅凭商店年龄分级推断玩家已经成年。开发测试与正式发布的年龄及同意配置须匹配实际受众。若当前构建不能确认适当的广告资格配置，保持广告关闭；正常游戏仍可使用。

儿童保护、权限与安全

本游戏的目标受众包括儿童。当前广告测试版对所有玩家统一采用儿童导向、未达同意年龄及 G 级广告内容设置，不启用个性化广告或再营销，不申请广告 ID、位置或通讯录权限。仅使用 Google AdMob，不接入第三方广告聚合。

广告入口提供无预选项的年龄段选择及“暂不提供，继续游戏”。年龄段仅保存在本机，用于管理广告入口，不上传出生日期或年龄段，也不影响正常游戏。未提供年龄段时不请求广告。

游戏无账号、云存档、真实货币购买或 Firebase Analytics。广告只用于可选的额外体力奖励。我们不会要求儿童提供姓名、电话或电子邮件；监护人可联系客服请求删除主动发送的资料。

应用使用系统私有目录保护本地记录；存档不宣称已加密。广告通信由 Google SDK 处理。儿童设置不表示广告完全不处理数据；具体处理情况和 SDK 信息见本政策。

English Privacy Policy

Scope and contact

This policy covers the Google edition of Endless Startrail (无尽星轨), provided by the individual developer 日生青. Contact: junyuy82@gmail.com. It does not replace the policy for the separate TapTap edition.

Information processed on your device

Level progress, equipment, chips, research, in-game currency, reward records, sound settings and acknowledgement of the local notice are stored in the app’s own directory to restore your game.

The game reads display dimensions, safe areas and system runtime information for graphics, input and audio. The offline edition does not automatically upload saves, gameplay records or logs to the developer.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any screenshots or logs you choose to attach. We use them to answer your request and investigate the issue. The game does not automatically send email or attachments. Do not send passwords, identity documents or unrelated sensitive information.

We restrict access to support information and retain it only while necessary to handle the request, investigate security issues or comply with legal obligations. You may request access, correction or deletion through the contact email.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains on the device until you clear app data, uninstall the app, remove the files or the operating system clears them. There is no game-operated cloud backup. Do not assume that updates, reinstalls or switching devices can restore progress.

On Android, use Settings → Apps → Endless Startrail → Storage → Clear data. This permanently removes local progress. Menu names differ by device. Uninstalling a Windows program may leave its save directory behind. Contact us if you need help identifying the correct folder.

The game does not create accounts, so there is no game-account deletion process.

This website

This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical access information, including IP addresses, for hosting and security under its privacy statement. The page adds no analytics scripts, advertising, forms or tracking cookies. Website access is separate from data processing by the game.

Applicable edition

This section applies only to Google builds offering optional Google AdMob rewarded advertising, including development builds using official test ads. Offline builds do not load these SDKs. This section replaces the offline policy’s no-advertising and no-network statements; the local-save, support and deletion provisions still apply.

Google advertising and privacy choices

Advertising builds use Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Privacy choices are checked before requesting ads, and Google consent forms are presented when required. You can decline to watch an ad and continue normal gameplay. The Advertising privacy choices entry in settings opens available privacy options.

Acknowledging the game notice is not advertising consent. Ads remain unavailable when permission to request ads cannot be established. Non-personalized ads can still process device, network and ad-interaction information.

SDK data and purposes

Depending on the device, region, SDK version and privacy choices, Google advertising services may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), advertising or other device/app identifiers, ad views and clicks, diagnostic and performance information, and consent choices. Purposes include serving and measuring advertising, fraud prevention and service operation. Advertising builds need network access.

We do not attach player names, email addresses, local saves or custom player IDs to ad requests. We do not enable Firebase Analytics or third-party ad mediation. SDK data is transmitted to Google and may be processed outside your country. Google’s policy explains its retention and privacy controls; deleting local saves does not necessarily delete information already processed by advertising services.

Rewards and controls

Rewarded ads appear only after you choose the reward button. Unavailable ads, playback failure, early exit or absence of a valid reward callback do not charge in-game currency or grant the ad reward. Natural stamina recovery and normal gameplay remain available.

Android advertising privacy settings may let you manage or delete advertising identifiers, depending on the OS version. Ad requests are subject to consent and other applicable privacy restrictions.

Children and eligibility

A store content rating alone is not treated as proof that a player is an adult. Advertising age and consent settings must match the intended audience. Builds without a confirmed appropriate advertising eligibility configuration keep advertising disabled while normal gameplay remains available.

Children, permissions and security

The intended audience includes children. This advertising test build applies child-directed treatment, under-age-of-consent treatment and a G ad-content limit to every player. Personalized advertising and remarketing are disabled; advertising-ID, location and contacts permissions are not requested. Only Google AdMob is used, without third-party mediation.

An age-band screen has no preselected answer and offers a skip option. The selected band stays on the device and controls the ad entry; no birth date or age band is sent to an ad request. Skipping does not restrict normal gameplay, and ads are not requested until a band has been provided.

There are no game accounts, cloud saves, real-money purchases or Firebase Analytics. Ads provide optional extra stamina. Children are not asked for names, phone numbers or email addresses. Guardians can contact support to request deletion of voluntarily submitted information.

Platform private storage helps protect local records; saves are not claimed to be encrypted. Google SDKs handle advertising communications. Child-directed settings do not mean that ads process no data.
